# Life Commander — Initial Pricing Model

## Positioning
A one-time purchase for a local-first personal productivity application.

## Initial price hypothesis

- Launch price: €19 / $19
- Standard price: €29 / $29
- Premium bundle: €39 / $39 if additional themes, templates and extended packs are included

These are hypotheses based on current comparable one-time digital planners and life-system products. They are not forecasts of sales.

## Why not €5-10?
That range is common for PDF planners and simple templates. A real offline application with local data, backup, multiple modules and five languages can be positioned above a static PDF.

## Why not €99 initially?
The product would need deeper functionality, integrations, automation, mature mobile behavior and a stronger brand before testing that price credibly.

## Suggested launch strategy

Version 1:
- €19 launch
- €29 regular

After strong product validation:
- €29 launch for major update
- €39 regular for expanded bundle

## Commercial promise

The product should be sold around:
"Buy once. Keep your personal productivity system."

Do not promise:
- guaranteed productivity improvement
- guaranteed financial results
- guaranteed sales
- lifetime cloud hosting

## Distribution candidates

- Gumroad: suitable for direct digital downloads and one-time payment.
- itch.io: suitable for downloadable digital products and software-like packages.
- Etsy: huge planner audience, but crowded and price-sensitive; application positioning should be clear.
- Own landing page can be added later, but it must not require our backend for the product itself.

## Revenue model

Customer pays once.
No mandatory subscription.
No per-user server cost.
Optional future paid upgrades can be considered, but V1 must be complete without them.
